# Poker Learning Layer

The learning layer turns the app from a poker simulator into a beginner-friendly coach.

## What It Explains

- Why a starting hand is strong, playable, or weak.
- Why pocket aces are powerful.
- Why hands like jack-two are risky.
- What suited cards actually mean.
- Why connected cards can make straights more easily.
- What a kicker is and why domination matters.
- What equity and pot odds mean in plain language.

## Beginner Explanation Principles

- Explain the reason behind a recommendation, not just the action.
- Use simple percentages only when they help.
- Separate raw winning chance from playability.
- Remind users that hidden opponent cards are unknown.
- Show that poker is about long-term expected value, not winning every single hand.

## Current UI Components

- AI Coach panel: action, equity, pot odds, confidence, and reasons.
- Your Hand panel: starting-hand strength, plain-English explanation, and common poker probabilities.
- Table Log: bot decisions and short strategic reasons.
- Seat action badges: latest visible bot move on the table.

## Future Improvements

- Board texture explanations after the flop.
- Draw explanations, such as flush draws and straight draws.
- Post-hand review comparing the user's action to the coach's recommendation.
- Opponent profile lessons, such as tight, loose, passive, and aggressive tendencies.
- Expert hand-history examples after the simulation pipeline exists.
